When you write that something cannot be done, does not exist, cannot be told apart, or has been searched exhaustively, put three things next to that sentence:

- 調べ方: the command, file, or source you checked
- 範囲: which data, conditions, and period that check covered
- 対照: something that does exist, or a changed condition, run through the same check, showing the check would have found it

If you cannot fill all three, do not write the negative claim. Write "not found by <調べ方> within <範囲>" and name what was not tried.
